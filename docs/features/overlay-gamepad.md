# Navigate the overlay with a controller

After a word lookup works with the pointer, open **Overlay Settings → Controller**. The controller pages cover setup, button bindings, navigation, advanced options, and devices and testing.

![Controller settings showing activation mode and the controller pages](/img/desktop/overlay-gamepad.png)

1. In **Setup**, enable gamepad navigation. Select **Modifier** to navigate while holding a button, or **Toggle** to enter and leave navigation mode.
2. Open **Devices & testing**, press a button and check that GSM receives input. On Windows, extended DirectInput / SDL support is available for additional devices.
3. In **Button bindings**, assign the modifier or toggle and your confirm and cancel actions. In **Navigation**, choose movement by character or token. For Japanese token selection, install the Sudachi dictionary offered in [setup](../getting-started/index.md).
4. With a line on screen, enter navigation mode and move to a word. Open its entry with confirm, or use auto-confirm to open entries as you move. Use the configured mining action to add a card and cancel to leave the popup.

Both [Yomitan and Hachidori](./overlay.md) work with controller navigation. Use the reader's own settings for its dictionaries and Anki deck.
